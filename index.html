Common questions

How does app blocking work?

You pick the apps, start a focus session, and iOS blocks them until the timer ends (or a budgeted break). It's built on Apple's Screen Time technology — we never see what apps you have installed.

How is my predicted ATAR calculated?

You enter your SAC scores and targets per subject; the app estimates your study scores, applies scaling, and converts your aggregate the same way VTAC does. It's a motivational estimate — not an official result, and we're not affiliated with VCAA or VTAC.

How do points and leaderboards work?

Points come from real work: focus sessions of 30 minutes or more with app blocking on (and breaks within limits), daily journals, tasks and reflections. Compete with friends, your school, or the global top 15 — leaderboards only ever show names and points, never marks.

What does Locked In (premium) include?

App blocking during focus sessions, unlimited SAC tracking per subject, appearance themes, full balance history and more. It's A$8.99/month with a 1-week free trial on monthly, or A$64.99/year.

Is my study data private?

Yes. Your scores, journal and academic data are never visible to other users — friends only ever see your name, username and points. Full details in our Privacy Policy.

Do breaks stop my study timer?

Yes — taking a break pauses the study countdown and starts a separate break timer while your apps unlock. When it runs out, apps re-lock and the study timer picks up exactly where it left off.

What year levels is it for?

VCE Year 11 and 12 — including Year 11s doing an accelerated 3/4 subject. Year 11s get their own setup so predictions still make sense.

Is there an Android version?

iOS only for now — app blocking is built on Apple's Screen Time, which has no Android equivalent. If enough people ask, an Android version (without blocking) is on the cards.

How do I cancel Locked In?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Atarly. Cancelling keeps your access until the end of the period.

Can I delete my account?

Anytime, in the app: Settings → Delete account. It permanently removes your account and data from our servers.
