3Personal information we collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide when using the App. All data is stored locally on your device. No data is sent to our servers in the current version of the App.

Profile & identity

  • Display name and year level (Year 11 or Year 12)
  • School name (optional, entered by you from a suggested list)
  • Profile photo (optional, chosen from your photo library)
  • Emoji avatar selection
  • Wake time and bedtime preferences
  • Personal goals you type in

Academic information

  • VCE subjects you are studying (subject codes)
  • Subject ranking (your strongest to weakest preference)
  • Target and desired study scores per subject
  • SAC scores, assessment weights, goals, and dates you enter
  • Accelerated (early-entry) subjects and their locked scores
  • Year 11 in-progress Unit 3&4 subjects
  • ATAR target you set
  • Strength bias preference

Task & schedule information

  • Homework tasks, assignments, and reminders you create
  • Due dates, times, priority levels, subject tags, and notes
  • Calendar events (title, category, date, start/end times, repeat settings)
  • Event difficulty ratings and kinds (SAC, exam, assignment)
  • Custom calendar categories you create

Study & productivity data

  • Study timer session logs (minutes studied per day)
  • Study streak days (dates you marked yourself as having studied)
  • Focus session data (duration, running state)
  • Study plan prep sessions recommended by the App

Wellbeing & journal data

  • Daily balance review ratings (sleep quality, life balance, study balance — rated by you each day)
  • Balance review comments you type
  • Journal entries (mood rating, productivity rating, free-text notes)
  • Daily check-in history and dates

Flashcards

  • Flashcard question and answer text you create, organised by subject

Gamification & social data

  • Study points and gems accumulated through App activity
  • Achievements unlocked and their dates
  • Friend display names, avatars, and points (stored locally; not shared externally in the current version)
  • Study groups you create (names and member lists, stored locally)
  • Referral code (auto-generated from your name — visible only to you)
  • Public/private profile preference

App preferences

  • Notification preferences (study reminders, streak reminders, SAC/exam alerts, daily motivation, bedtime wind-down)
  • Blocked apps list (identifiers of apps you chose to block during focus — not transmitted to us)
  • Accent theme selection
  • App synced status flag

Technical information (diagnostic only)

  • App version, device model, iOS version — collected only if a crash occurs
  • General usage patterns (which screens are visited, aggregated and anonymised)

We do not collect financial information, government identifiers (e.g. TFN, Medicare number), precise GPS location, health records, or any sensitive information as defined under the Australian Privacy Act 1988.

4How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely for the following purposes:

  • To operate and deliver all App features: ATAR calculator, SAC tracker, task manager, exam countdown, in-app schedule, study timer, balance reviews, journal, flashcards, achievements, social leaderboard, and study plan generator
  • To calculate your predicted ATAR using your subject rankings and study scores
  • To generate personalised study plan recommendations before SACs and exams
  • To track your study streak, timer logs, and progress toward achievements
  • To send push notifications you have opted into (reminders, alerts, and motivational messages)
  • To restore your settings and data if you delete and reinstall the App (local device only)
  • To improve the App through anonymised, aggregated usage analytics
  • To diagnose and fix crashes using anonymised device diagnostics
  • To manage your Premium subscription (if applicable) through Apple's in-app purchase system

We do not use your information for targeted advertising, behavioural profiling, or any purpose that is incompatible with the above. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party under any circumstances.

5Photo library access

If you choose to set a profile picture, the App requests access to your device's photo library.

  • Your selected photo is saved exclusively on your device
  • We do not upload, transmit, process, or share your photos in any way
  • Photo access is used only at the moment you select or change a photo
  • You can change or remove your profile photo at any time in Settings

To revoke access at any time: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → ATAR Maxing → set to "None".

This disclosure complies with Apple's requirements for photo library access.

6Push notifications

The App requests permission to send push notifications. If permission is granted, we may send the following types of notifications:

  • Study reminders: nudges for planned study blocks on your schedule
  • Streak reminders: daily prompts to maintain your study streak
  • SAC & exam deadline alerts: heads-up before assessments based on dates you entered
  • Daily motivation: to-do reminders and motivational messages
  • Bedtime wind-down: a nightly reminder (approximately 1 hour before your set bedtime) to complete your daily balance check-in

All notifications are scheduled locally on your device using your bedtime and schedule data. We do not use third-party push notification services that link notification data to your identity.

To opt out of all notifications: iOS Settings → Notifications → ATAR Maxing → toggle off "Allow Notifications".
To manage individual notification types: Settings → Notifications within the App.

Disabling notifications does not affect access to any App features.

7Screen Time and app blocking (Focus Mode)

ATAR Maxing includes an optional Focus Mode feature that restricts access to distracting apps while you study. This feature uses Apple's Family Controls and Managed Settings framework, which requires a special entitlement granted by Apple.

When you use Focus Mode:

  • You personally choose which apps to block from within the App — we do not see, read, or store the names or identifiers of all apps installed on your device
  • We store only the list of apps YOU selected to block (the "blocked apps" list in your settings)
  • App restrictions are enforced entirely by iOS — no usage data is transmitted to us
  • You can remove all restrictions at any time from within ATAR Maxing or from iOS Settings → Screen Time
  • Focus sessions are timed and require your explicit activation — we do not enable restrictions automatically

This feature complies with Apple's requirements for the Family Controls entitlement.

8In-app schedule and calendar

The App includes a full in-app schedule and timetable. All calendar events, categories, and schedule data are stored within the App on your device only.

  • We do not request access to Apple Calendar
  • We do not read from or write to your device's native Calendar or Reminders apps
  • We do not sync schedule data to any external calendar service

If a future update introduces optional Apple Calendar integration, we will request your explicit permission at that time and update this Policy before activating that feature.

9Journal entries and mood data

The App includes an optional daily journal feature where you can record your mood rating, productivity rating, and free-text notes.

  • Journal entries are stored locally on your device only
  • This data is never transmitted, uploaded, or shared with us or any third party
  • Journal data is used only to display your history within the App and to contribute to your balance score and achievement tracking
  • You can delete individual entries or all journal data at any time via Settings → Delete all my data

We treat journal and mood data with the highest level of privacy. It is never used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose outside of the App's wellbeing features.

10Social features, leaderboard, and referral codes

ATAR Maxing includes a social leaderboard that shows study points and a friends list. In the current version, all leaderboard and friend data is stored locally on your device and is not shared with any server or other users.

Referral codes: The App generates a referral code from your display name (e.g. "ALEX26"). This code is shown to you in Settings and can be shared to invite friends. No referral data is transmitted to our servers in the current version.

In a planned future version, we intend to introduce real-time social connections (adding friends by username and sharing leaderboard data). Before this feature is activated:

  • You will be asked to create an account with a chosen username
  • Only your username and study points total will be visible to other users
  • Your real name, SAC scores, ATAR estimate, journal entries, mood data, and all academic data will never be shared with other users without your explicit consent
  • You will be able to set your profile to private at any time
  • You will be able to delete your account and all server-side data
  • This Policy will be updated and you will be notified before any network-connected social features are activated

We will never share identifiable academic or personal data with other users.

11Study timer and study log

The App includes a study timer (Focus Session) that tracks how long you study. When you complete a study session:

  • The duration (in minutes) is recorded against today's date in a local study log
  • This data is used to calculate achievements, display study history, and track your streak
  • The study log is stored locally on your device only
  • No study session content, screen activity, or app usage during your session is monitored or recorded

12Achievements and gamification

ATAR Maxing includes an achievements system with study points (gems). Achievements are earned by reaching milestones such as study streaks, SAC scores, task completion, and daily check-ins.

  • Achievement progress, unlock dates, and points are calculated locally from your App data
  • Points totals are displayed on the leaderboard (locally in the current version)
  • No gamification data is transmitted to external servers in the current version
  • When cloud accounts are introduced (future), your points and achievements will be linked to your account with your consent

13ATAR prediction disclaimer

ATAR estimates shown in this App are mathematical models based solely on the data you enter and publicly available VTAC scaling coefficients. They are provided for informational and motivational purposes only.

ATAR Maxing makes no warranty, express or implied, that any predicted ATAR will be achieved. Actual ATAR outcomes are determined by VCAA and VTAC through processes including exam marking, cohort scaling, and statistical moderation that cannot be predicted in advance.

Do not make educational, enrolment, or career decisions based solely on estimates shown in this App. Always refer to official VCAA and VTAC publications for authoritative information.

Exam dates displayed in the App are approximations based on historical VCAA scheduling patterns. Always verify official exam dates at vcaa.vic.edu.au.

We are not affiliated with VCAA, VTAC, the Department of Education Victoria, ACARA, or any tertiary institution.

16Data storage and security

Current architecture — fully local
All personal data is stored on your device using AsyncStorage, which is protected by your device's own security features (Face ID, Touch ID, passcode). No personal data is transmitted to our servers in the current version of the App.

Future cloud sync (planned)
If we introduce optional cloud backup or account sync in a future update:

  • You will be asked to explicitly opt in — it will never be automatic
  • Data will be transmitted over HTTPS using TLS encryption
  • Stored data will be encrypted at rest on our servers
  • This Policy will be updated before any server-side storage is activated, and you will be notified in-app

Our security recommendations

  • Keep your iOS version up to date
  • Enable Face ID, Touch ID, or a device passcode
  • Do not share your device with others if you have sensitive academic data in the App

Data breach notification
We will notify you of any data breach that is likely to result in serious harm to your personal information, as required under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme (Privacy Act 1988, Part IIIC).
